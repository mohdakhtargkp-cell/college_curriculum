ABES Engineering College

ABES Engineering College is a reputed engineering college located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The college was established in 2000 and became an Autonomous College in 2025.

About ABES

ABES Engineering College focuses on academic excellence, innovation and industry-ready learning. The college provides various undergraduate and postgraduate programmes for students.
